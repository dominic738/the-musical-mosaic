## Scope

`web/index.html`: the whole Musical Mosaic app, one screen. The live mosaic leads; the sidebar serves it.

## Audience and job

Friends of the owner, alone on a laptop or phone, playing with the live mosaic and making photo strips to share.

## Direction

A sibling of The Musical Compass (mymusicalcompass.com, `../compass`): its sidebar-and-stage shell, Arimo, square black-bordered chrome and white paper, with all color coming from the album covers and a live accent from the album you are mostly made of. See DESIGN.md for tokens.
