# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS in `web/`, no build step, hosted on GitHub Pages. Assets (`web/data/atlas.jpg`, `web/data/albums.json`) are generated by `build_web_assets.py` from the Python pipeline.

## Users

Friends the owner sends the link to. They try it alone on a laptop or phone, for fun, and share the photo strips they make.

## Product Purpose

The Musical Mosaic turns a live webcam feed into a mosaic of album covers: every tile is the album whose average colour is closest to that patch of the frame. Visitors play with it live and take a four-pose photo strip to keep or share. Success is people taking strips and sending them on.

## Positioning

The default crate is the owner's own taste: 2,184 albums from their "Good Songs" Spotify playlist, so visitors are literally rebuilt out of the owner's records. Visitors can swap in their own Spotify library instead.

## Operating Context

Webcam in a browser, usually indoors at a laptop or on a phone. Video never leaves the browser. Strips are downloaded as PNGs and shared elsewhere.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Live mosaic with adjustable tiles across, mirror, pause, save still, flip camera.
- Hover a tile to see the album; click to open it in Spotify.
- "You're made of" list of the albums covering most of the frame.
- Photobooth: countdown, four shots, printed strip with download/discard.
- Crates: "Good Songs" (owner's playlist) or "Your Spotify" (PKCE login; Spotify dev mode limits logins to allowlisted users).
- Layout: mosaic stage plus a permanent side panel that can be hidden.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **The Musical Mosaic** (replaces "Album Filter").
- Styled as a sibling of the owner's other site, The Musical Compass (mymusicalcompass.com, repo ../compass): same Arimo type, black-on-white square chrome, grey numbered sidebar. Color comes from the album covers. Rejected along the way: blue-and-yellow booth, "Station Tile" (tacky), plain dark neutral (emotionless).

## Evidence on Hand

Album covers and metadata from the owner's playlist (`album_colors.json`, `albums.json`, `web/data/`). No testimonials, users or stats exist; none may be invented.

## Product Principles

- The mosaic is the show; the interface serves it.
- It's personal: the crate is someone's real record collection.
- Making and sharing a strip is the payoff.
- Privacy is plain: the camera stays in the browser.
